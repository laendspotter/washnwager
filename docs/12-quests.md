# Section 18: Quest System (50 Quests)

Quests are tracked in the **Quest Board** (newspaper icon in the top bar). Active quests: up to 5 simultaneously. 
Each quest has a name, funny description, objective, and reward.
Quests are grouped by type and roughly ordered by when they unlock.

Format: **Name** | *"Funny description."* | Objective | Reward
