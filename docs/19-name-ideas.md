## Final Note

**WASH & WAGER** was chosen because:
- **Wash** = the cover story (laundromat, cleaning, everything above board)
- **Wager** = the truth (the backroom, the risk, the sitcom chaos)
- Together: alliterative, two syllables each, memorable at a glance, says exactly
  what the game is without saying anything that can get anyone in trouble
- Works as a laundromat name *and* as a game title simultaneously
- Retta would approve. She'd put it on the sign. She did put it on the sign.
