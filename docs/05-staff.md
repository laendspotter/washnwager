# Section 9: Staff System

Retta can't be everywhere at once (though she tries). Staff are hired from the Staff menu,
assigned to specific roles, and upgraded through 5 tiers. Each staff member has a
personality that shows up in dialogue events. They can also say funny things during
Heat events, customer interactions, and chapter beats.

**Hiring cost** = one-time fee.
**Weekly wage** = ongoing cost (deducted from the daily idle earnings automatically).
**Upgrade cost** = one-time, increases with each tier.

---

## Staff Role 1: The Cleaner — "Wanda Pickle"

**Job:** Wanda mops spills, cleans bathrooms, empties trash cans, wipes down machines,
and generally keeps the front laundromat from looking like a disaster zone. Without her,
the player handles every spill manually.

**Hire cost:** $150 one-time
**Weekly wage:** $80/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Wanda cleans one spill per minute. Bathroom cleaned every 10 min. Occasionally
  misses a spill. Carries one mop. Visual: tired but functional.
- **L2:** Wanda cleans spills in 30 seconds. Takes initiative (no tap needed for obvious spills).
  Carries a color-coded cleaning kit.
- **L3:** Wanda cleans two spills simultaneously (bucket + spray). Adds window-cleaning to
  her route. Visual: professional uniform.
- **L4:** Wanda adds a "Hygiene Boost" aura: nearby customers are 15% more patient.
  She also pre-empts lint trap fullness by checking dryers proactively.
- **L5:** Wanda runs a full cleaning circuit every 3 minutes, touching every surface.
  A small sparkle animation follows her route. Customer satisfaction from cleanliness
  is permanently +20.

**Strengths:** Never complains about mopping. Knows where everything is.
**Weaknesses:** Doesn't repair machines. Ignores the backroom unless specifically assigned.
Has a territorial relationship with the mop bucket.

**Funny personality:** Wanda is deeply invested in the concept of a clean floor and
regards every spill as a personal affront. She names her mops. The current one is Gerald.
She does not explain this.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"If someone spills coffee on my floor one more time I will personally install cup holders."*
- *"Lint trap's full again. You'd think these machines didn't have owners."*
- *"I am not cleaning that. That's a dryer sheet soaked in… I don't know what that is. Denny!"*

## Staff Role 2: The Repair Guy — "Denny Briggs"

**Job:** Denny repairs broken washing machines, dryers, coin machines, and eventually
backroom equipment. Without him, every machine repair falls to Retta.

**Hire cost:** $200 one-time
**Weekly wage:** $100/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Denny repairs one machine at a time. Repair takes 20 seconds (vs. Retta's 12s,
  but frees Retta up). Occasionally repairs the wrong thing first.
- **L2:** Repair time reduced to 12 seconds. Denny self-initiates (no tap required for broken
  machines in his route). Can repair coin machine jams.
- **L3:** Denny upgrades machine durability passively — all front machines break 20% less
  often. He carries his own parts kit.
- **L4:** Denny can maintain backroom machines (slots, lucky wheel) if assigned. He does not
  ask questions about the backroom. This is his most appreciated quality.
- **L5:** Denny performs "preventive maintenance" — a weekly check that halves breakdown
  probability for the next 7 days. Visual: full toolbelt, safety goggles.

**Strengths:** Reliable, quiet, doesn't gossip, genuinely good at his job.
**Weaknesses:** Slow to prioritize without direction. Will sometimes get absorbed in
a particularly interesting repair and forget the world exists.

**Funny personality:** Denny believes every machine problem can be solved with the right
screwdriver and the right amount of patience. He talks to the machines while he works.
He insists they respond.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"Washer 3 is acting up again. She needs time. I'll talk her through it."*
- *"What is this? (holds up unidentifiable object) How did this get inside the drum? 
  What IS this?"*
- *"I don't ask what's in the backroom. I fix things. That's the deal."*

## Staff Role 3: The Cashier — "Terri Fawn"

**Job:** Terri staffs the front counter, handles customer questions and complaints,
processes the coin machine efficiently, and collects tips. With Terri at the counter,
Retta can move freely without abandoning the front desk.

**Hire cost:** $250 one-time
**Weekly wage:** $120/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Handles basic counter interactions. Calms angry customers 50% of the time (vs.
  Retta's 90%). Collects tip jar automatically.
- **L2:** Calms customers 75% of the time. Unlocks "counter upsell": when customers check
  out, Terri suggests detergent or dryer sheets, boosting average spend by 8%.
- **L3:** Terri handles the coin machine without prompting. Customer wait-time tolerance
  increases 10% (she's good at managing expectations).
- **L4:** Terri gains "Regulars Knowledge" — she remembers repeat customers' preferences and
  greets them by name (+5% tip from regulars per visit).
- **L5:** Terri runs the front completely autonomously. Retta's presence at the counter is
  no longer required during normal operations. Terri also screens suspicious visitors
  before they reach the back ("full lobby control").

**Strengths:** Social, likeable, great with grumpy customers.
**Weaknesses:** Can't repair or clean. Occasionally chats too long with customers,
slowing their checkout by 10 seconds.

**Funny personality:** Terri is relentlessly cheerful in a way that most people find
pleasant and the Impatient Businessman finds infuriating. She has a laugh that can be
heard from the parking lot.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"Good morning, welcome to Retta's! Everything is working perfectly and if it isn't,
  I promise we're aware of it."*
- *"Sir, I understand your frustration, and I want you to know that I personally care
  very deeply about your dryer cycle."*
- *"No, that's the storage room. The storage room has storage in it. That's what it's for."*

## Staff Role 4: The Vending Restocker — "Hector Lima"

**Job:** Hector monitors and restocks all vending machines, the detergent shelf, the
snack corner, and (eventually) the backroom snack bar. Without him, these all require
manual player taps.

**Hire cost:** $150 one-time
**Weekly wage:** $70/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Hector restocks vending machines when depleted (triggered by tap or self-check
  every 5 minutes). Brings one item type per trip.
- **L2:** Hector does proactive checks. He restocks at 25% capacity (before depletion).
  Also manages the detergent shelf.
- **L3:** Hector optimizes stock based on what sells: he orders more of the high-sellers,
  boosting vending income by 15%.
- **L4:** Hector can manage the snack corner and backroom snack bar simultaneously.
  Backroom snack revenue increases 20% when he's assigned back there.
- **L5:** Hector adds "Premium Items" to the rotation (higher-end snacks and drinks) that
  sell at double margin. Total vending/snack income +35%.

**Strengths:** Never forgets what needs restocking. Surprisingly fast mover.
**Weaknesses:** Clueless about everything else — if a machine breaks right next to him,
he'll politely step around it and continue stocking chips.

**Funny personality:** Hector treats snack supply chain logistics with the seriousness
of a military operation. He has a clipboard. He checks things off.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"Honey BBQ Puffs down to two bags. I'm calling it a Code Yellow. I need to inform Retta."*
- *"Back room is requesting more sweet tea. I am not going to ask why they need that much
  sweet tea. I am just going to provide it."*
- *"Detergent shelf looks a little thin. I'm on it."*

## Staff Role 5: The Floor Manager — "Big Lorene"

**Job:** Big Lorene is Retta's second-in-command for the front laundromat. She coordinates
staff assignments, redirects customers to open machines, mediates customer disputes,
and ensures the floor runs smoothly during busy periods.

**Hire cost:** $350 one-time (available from Chapter 3+)
**Weekly wage:** $150/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Lorene redirects 2 customers per minute to open machines (reducing wait times by 15%).
  Mediates basic complaints so Retta doesn't have to stop what she's doing.
- **L2:** Lorene manages staff task queues: she'll send Wanda to a spill and Denny to a
  broken machine without player input.
- **L3:** Lorene handles all front-room customer complaints autonomously (except major events).
  Her "authority presence" reduces customer anger escalation by 30%.
- **L4:** Lorene can run "Efficiency Drills" — once per hour, she optimizes machine usage
  patterns, temporarily boosting throughput by 20%.
- **L5:** Lorene's "Manager Mode": during high-traffic events (Jubilee, special events),
  she double-staffs response times and reduces all front-room wait times by 25%.

**Strengths:** Great at the big picture. Calm under pressure. Everyone respects her.
**Weaknesses:** She's expensive. She also sometimes over-manages Denny, who resents it quietly.

**Funny personality:** Big Lorene has managed a school cafeteria for 15 years and a
Chuck E. Cheese for two. Nothing frightens her. Nothing.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"You there! Machine 4 is free. Go. Don't wander. Machine 4."*
- *"Denny! That machine has been blinking for six minutes. Why are you eating chips?"*
- *"Sir, we have a process here. The process is: coins go in, clothes go in, you wait.
  Step two. Are you on step two?"*

## Staff Role 6: The Security Guy — "Bubba Crane"

**Job:** Bubba is a gentle giant who stands near the front and/or backroom entrance,
deters trouble, manages unruly customers, and handles the occasional "escort out of the
building" situation. He also has an important secondary function: intimidating nosy visitors.

**Hire cost:** $300 one-time (available from Chapter 5+)
**Weekly wage:** $130/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Bubba's presence alone reduces Sore Loser escalation events by 50%. Can physically
  escort one disruptive customer per session.
- **L2:** Bubba monitors the alley entrance. He recognizes regulars and waves them in without
  a knock. Strangers get checked.
- **L3:** Bubba can "intercept" the first heat-raising visitor each chapter (sends them away
  with a credible story that Retta gives him via radio).
- **L4:** Bubba gets a radio and lookout camera access. He warns Retta 5 seconds earlier
  than the camera alone when a suspicious person approaches.
- **L5:** Bubba Mode: during a Heat spike above 60, Bubba activates "Emergency Protocol" —
  he locks the alley, moves three backroom regulars to the fake storage room, and covers
  all visible gambling items with laundry. He does this *automatically*. Heat drops 15.

**Strengths:** Intimidating without being aggressive. Loyal. Has a photographic memory for faces.
**Weaknesses:** He is too nice to be actually scary. Cletus once walked past him three times
without being stopped because Bubba didn't want to seem rude.

**Funny personality:** Bubba is a softie. He has a cat named Duchess. He offers everyone who
leaves a parting fist bump. He cries at bingo nights.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"Evening. You're new. Who sent you? …Roy? Okay. Roy's good people. Go on in."*
- *"Sir, I'm going to need you to stop doing that. Please stop. Sir. I'm asking nicely."*
- *"(quiet voice) Retta, camera 2 shows someone who looks like either Gerald Finch
  or Gerald Finch's twin. I'm hoping for the twin."*

## Staff Role 7: The Backroom Host — "Carlotta Reyes"

**Job:** Carlotta runs the backroom. She collects buy-ins, explains rules to new players,
manages the bingo call rotation, deals poker when needed, and keeps the energy in the room
warm, funny, and not-too-loud. She is the backroom's Terri.

**Hire cost:** $400 one-time (available from Chapter 5+)
**Weekly wage:** $180/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Carlotta manages backroom customer check-ins. Reduces "confused new player" events
  by 70%. She handles basic bingo runs.
- **L2:** Carlotta upsells premium seating, extra cards, and drinks. Backroom income +12%.
- **L3:** Carlotta introduces "House Specials" — creative game variations that bring in an
  extra $150/session.
- **L4:** Carlotta gains "People Management" — she handles Sore Loser events herself (90%
  success rate), sending them out warmly before things escalate.
- **L5:** Carlotta mode: the backroom operates fully autonomously. Retta doesn't need to
  monitor the backroom during normal sessions. Carlotta also signals Heat-raising events
  to Bubba pre-emptively.

**Strengths:** Bilingual (talks to everyone). Graceful under pressure. Never raises her voice.
**Weaknesses:** Can't handle mechanical issues (slot malfunction, etc.) without Denny.

**Funny personality:** Carlotta genuinely loves her job. She considers herself an events
coordinator, not a casino host. She sends birthday bingo cards to regulars.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"Welcome to The Lint Trap. I'll need $20 buy-in, first name only, and if anyone asks,
  this is a sewing circle."*
- *"Mr. Boone, I am going to need you to express your joy at a volume that only humans
  within this room can hear. Can you do that for me?"*
- *"Roy, honey, I know it's been a rough night. Sit here. I'll get you a sweet tea and
  a fresh deck."*

## Staff Role 8: The Lookout — "Kevin 'Kev' Purdle"

**Job:** Kev monitors the lookout camera system, watches the alley entrance and front window,
and gives Retta advance warning of suspicious visitors (cops, inspectors, nosy neighbors).
He is the early warning system.

**Hire cost:** $200 one-time (available from Chapter 6+)
**Weekly wage:** $90/week

**Levels:**
- **L1:** Kev watches the single camera. 3-second warning before a suspicious visitor arrives.
  Texts a warning emoji to Retta's in-game phone.
- **L2:** Kev watches 2 cameras simultaneously (front door + alley). 6-second warning.
  Can identify known recurring threats (Clem, Gerald, Connie) by face.
- **L3:** Kev extends warning time to 10 seconds. Adds a "suspicion rating" to each person
  (Low / Medium / High) so Retta knows how to respond.
- **L4:** Kev monitors social media and local gossip channels. If Connie posts something
  relevant, Kev flags it. Heat events from gossip are pre-warned 1 full minute early.
- **L5:** Kev installs a 4-camera system with motion detection. Any person approaching the
  building triggers a notification. Warning time: up to 20 seconds. He also learns Clem's
  patrol schedule and can predict when he's coming.

**Strengths:** Observant, tech-savvy, never away from his post.
**Weaknesses:** Socially awkward everywhere that isn't a camera screen. Refuses to go into
the backroom because "it makes him feel like he's on the wrong side of the camera."

**Funny personality:** Kev names the regulars on the cameras. Clem is "Eagle One." Gerald
is "Clipboard." Connie is simply "Incoming."

**Select dialogue:**
- *"Retta. Retta! Eagle One is in the parking lot. Repeat: Eagle One is in the parking lot.
  He has a bag of laundry. He's coming in. He looks normal. He is not normal."*
- *"Incoming at the front. It's Clipboard. He has a new clipboard. I repeat: new clipboard."*
- *"Front camera picked up Wade's car slowing down outside for the third time this week.
  He just drove past again. He is definitely doing what I think he's doing."*

## Staff Role 9: Grandma's Helper — "Earl Jr."

**Job:** Earl Jr. is Retta's anxious son-in-law. He didn't sign up for this. He is
technically unpaid (family). He shows up twice a chapter to check on "his investment"
and can be directed to help with tasks. He is not good at them but he's trying.

**Hire cost:** $0 (comes with the story)
**Weekly wage:** $0 (family; deeply regrets this)

**Levels:**
- **L1 (Chapter 1–3):** Earl Jr. helps with basic tasks if told to. 60% success rate. Drops
  things occasionally. Useful as a distraction for nosy visitors.
- **L2 (Chapter 4–5):** Earl Jr. finds out about the backroom. He is horrified. He decides
  to help anyway because "I paid for that building." He can now do basic cover-up tasks.
- **L3 (Chapter 6–7):** Earl Jr. stops being horrified and starts being strategic. He develops
  a cover story and can deliver it to inspectors with reasonable conviction.
- **L4 (Chapter 8–9):** Earl Jr. becomes "Compliance Officer" — he handles all of Gerald
  Finch's health inspection paperwork autonomously, freeing Retta from that stress entirely.
- **L5 (Chapter 10):** Earl Jr. has a moment at the Jubilee. He gives a small speech about
  believing in people. He cries. Retta pats his arm. He is now fully on Team Retta.

**Strengths:** Free labor. Genuinely cares. Surprisingly good in a crisis once committed.
**Weaknesses:** Nervous, drops things, very bad liar in early chapters.

**Funny personality:** Earl Jr.'s entire arc is a man who came in to protect his investment
and ended up protecting his family. He calls Retta "Ma" by Chapter 9. She hasn't corrected him.

**Select dialogue:**
- *"This is fine. I'm fine. The machines are fine. Is that a poker chip?!"*
- *"The health inspector asked me what the back room is for and I said 'storage' and
  he asked storage of what and I said 'things' and he wrote something down."*
- *"(to the health inspector, Chapter 8) Sir, I have the complete maintenance documentation
  for every machine in this facility, cross-referenced with local code, current as of this
  morning. Would you like to see it? I have copies."*
