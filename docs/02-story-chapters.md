# Section 4: Chapter-Based Story Progression

**Setting:** Peavine, Texas. Population 4,200. One stoplight. Two churches. Zero secrets
(theoretically).

**Protagonist:** Loretta "Retta" Hollis, 72, retired schoolteacher, widow of Earl Hollis
(rest his soul and his casseroles). Sharp, funny, stubborn, and dangerously bored.

---

## CHAPTER 1 — "The Rusty Drum"
### *Buying the Laundromat*

**Story Setup:**
Retta's daughter Patrice keeps suggesting "senior living communities." Retta responds by
spending most of her savings on a condemned laundromat on Gipson Street. The place hasn't
been properly cleaned since the Clinton administration. The sign out front reads
"Old Dewey's Wash-O-Rama" in half-lit neon. Two of the four washers work. The dryer smells
like a goat. Retta is thrilled.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Tutorial phase.** Player learns tap-to-collect coins, tap-to-repair broken machines,
tap-to-mop spills. First customers trickle in. Patience meters are very forgiving.

**New Room:**
The full starting laundromat (see Layout Section 5). All rooms are present but most upgrades
are at Level 1 / broken state.

**New NPC:**
**Earl Jr.** — Retta's nervous son-in-law who "lent" her the down payment and regrets it.
He visits twice a chapter to check on the investment and is visibly panicking at all times.

**Conflict:**
Two of the four washers are broken on Day 1. The coin machine is jammed. A customer is
already complaining loudly before the door is fully open.

**Funny Event:**
*"The Handoff"* — Previous owner Dewey shows up for a "quick walkthrough" and hands Retta
a manual that is entirely crabbed handwriting in pencil. Key entry: *"Washer 3 — hit it
on the left side when the spin cycle stutters. If that don't work, yell at it."*

**Chapter Goal:**
- Fix both broken washers.
- Fix the coin machine.
- Serve 20 customers.
- Earn $500 total revenue.

**Reward:**
- Unlock the **Detergent Shelf** (sell detergent directly).
- Unlock the **Vending Machine** slot.
- Retta's first ability: **Bless Your Heart** (calms one angry customer, no cooldown in Ch.1).
- Title card: *"Retta's Wash & Wager — Now Open (Mostly)"*

## CHAPTER 2 — "Open For Business"
### *Opening Day Chaos*

**Story Setup:**
Word gets out that someone bought the old Wash-O-Rama and fixed it up. Half of Peavine comes
in out of curiosity. The other half comes in because they desperately need laundry done.
It's louder, messier, and more profitable than Retta expected. She also finally reads the
back page of Dewey's manual: *"Storage room in back — door sticks. Don't open if you got
company."* She makes a mental note.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Customer patience system fully activates.** Customers now leave if they wait too long.
**Vending machine** comes online — restocking added to the task loop. **Tip jar** at counter
starts generating passive income.

**New Room:**
**Waiting Lounge corner** (two bench tiles added to the entrance area). Customers who wait
here are 25% more patient.

**New NPC:**
**Connie Dupree** — the town gossip. Shows up on Day 3, does one load of laundry, and
spends the rest of the time walking very slowly around the building staring at everything.
She becomes a recurring rival. Her presence slightly raises the Heat meter later.

**Conflict:**
Three customers arrive at once, all with massive laundry loads. One washer breaks. Retta
has exactly one pair of hands. Player must prioritize: repair the washer or calm the three
impatient customers?

**Funny Event:**
*"The Sock Investigation"* — A retired military man named **Horace Wembley** arrives with
one sock and demands to know what happened to the other one. Retta has to either blame
"the machine" (lose 10 reputation), find him a spare sock from lost and found (gain 5 charm),
or tell him socks come in pairs for a reason and move on (neutral, funny dialogue).

**Chapter Goal:**
- Serve 50 customers.
- Earn $1,500 total revenue.
- Complete the waiting lounge bench upgrade.
- Keep customer satisfaction above 70% for 2 in-game days.

**Reward:**
- Unlock **Coin Auto-Collector** (basic tier — collects front room coins automatically).
- Unlock the **Repair Guy** staff slot (hire Denny Briggs, costs $200/week).
- Discover the mysterious storage room door. Retta wiggles the handle. A low, strange noise
  comes from the other side. She decides to open it next chapter.

## CHAPTER 3 — "Regular Rascals"
### *Building the Regulars*

**Story Setup:**
The laundromat has found its rhythm. The same faces come back every week. The construction
crew. Old Mrs. Pettibone with her cat-hair sweaters. The college kid from the tech school
who somehow always has exactly one dollar less than he needs. Retta knows all their names.
She also bought a new padlock for the storage room door. The old one had been knocked off
from the inside. *The inside.*

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Customer archetype bonuses** activate: regulars who visit 5+ times start leaving bigger
tips. **Reputation bar** begins tracking. Decor upgrades (plants, a better sign, floor tiles)
become available and directly affect customer patience and spending.

**New Room:**
**Front counter / small office** gets upgraded: Retta can now unlock a simple ledger system
that shows daily revenue breakdowns.

**New NPC:**
**Roy Macabee** — quiet, friendly, perpetually unlucky. He comes in every Tuesday with
exactly three loads of laundry and mentions he used to play cards in the back of Dewey's
old place. He always says it the same way: *"Good times. Real good times."* He eyes the
storage room every visit.

**Conflict:**
Wade Pruett is seen across the street taking photos of Retta's building. He's planning to
open a rival laundromat. He's also Connie's cousin. The two of them at once could be trouble.

**Funny Event:**
*"The Lucky Chip"* — A 10-year-old kid finds a poker chip under Washer 2 while retrieving a
lost sneaker. He asks Retta what it is. Player choices:
- A: *"That's a souvenir from the county fair."* (Neutral, kid buys it)
- B: *"Put it back, honey."* (Kid drops it, +Heat 2)
- C: Call it a "special laundry token" and trade the kid a candy bar for it. (-$1, +5 charm)

**Chapter Goal:**
- Earn 10 regulars (customers who return 3+ times).
- Upgrade one washer to Level 2.
- Upgrade the floor tiles.
- Reach 80% customer satisfaction.

**Reward:**
- **Roy Macabee** officially joins the recurring cast.
- Storage room door opens during a cutscene: Retta pushes through and finds a dusty card
  table, two mismatched chairs, a dead houseplant, and an ashtray shaped like Texas.
- Unlock: **Chapter 4 teaser dialogue** — Roy materializes beside Retta and says,
  *"Well now. I always wondered if this room was still here."*

## CHAPTER 4 — "Something in the Back"
### *The First Card Table*

**Story Setup:**
Retta cleans up the storage room. She doesn't tell anyone. She keeps the door locked. She
tells her son-in-law Earl Jr. it's "overflow inventory." She tells the cleaner it's "being
renovated." She tells Connie there's asbestos. Two weeks later, Roy Macabee is playing cards
back there every Tuesday with three friends. Retta provides sweet tea and charges a "cleaning
fee" of $10 per person per session. It's completely above board. She has a sign.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Backroom loop begins.** A new "Back Room" tab appears in the navigation. Backroom customers
arrive via the alley entrance (a separate spawn point). **Heat Meter** activates at 0.
First heat event triggers if the Heat reaches 15. **Sweet Tea income** is small but passive.

**New Room:**
**The Storage Room / Proto-Backroom** (6×8 tile space behind the laundromat main room):
- 1× Card table
- 2× Mismatched chairs
- 1× "Totally Normal Storage" shelf (fake cover)
- 1× Sweet tea pitcher (Retta's station)
- 1× Exit to alley

**New NPC:**
**Darlene Hooks** — bingo enthusiast and Roy's neighbor. Roy brings her along one night.
She doesn't play cards. She brings a bingo dauber and a personal card "just in case." She
asks Retta when they're going to do bingo. *"Soon, hon, soon,"* says Retta.

**Conflict:**
The alley entrance isn't hidden well yet. Connie Dupree spots Roy walking in through the
alley carrying folding chairs. She tells Pastor Wendell. He shows up the next Sunday to
ask Retta if she needs help "organizing community activities." He volunteers the church
fellowship hall. Retta thanks him profusely and says she'll keep it in mind.

**Funny Event:**
*"Secret Knock Disaster"* — Retta establishes a secret knock for the backroom door
(three knocks, pause, two knocks). Roy forgets it and starts banging in rhythm to a country
song. The whole front laundromat can hear it. Player choices:
- A: Open the door quickly and pull Roy in. (–Heat 5 immediately, Roy is embarrassed)
- B: Pretend it's a plumbing problem and send Denny the repair guy to "check the pipes."
  (Neutral, confused customers, funny dialogue)
- C: Yell *"Who's there?"* through the wall as if you have no idea what's happening.
  (+Heat 3, best comedy outcome)

**Chapter Goal:**
- Host 5 card game sessions.
- Earn $500 from backroom income.
- Keep Heat below 20.
- Upgrade the fake storage shelf.

**Reward:**
- Unlock **Lookout Camera** (spots suspicious visitors approaching before they arrive).
- Darlene and Roy are now Tier 1 backroom regulars.
- **Bingo Board Blueprint** unlocks (can purchase in Chapter 5).
- New Retta ability: **Hush Now** (silences backroom sounds for 60 seconds).

## CHAPTER 5 — "Bingo Bonanza"
### *Bingo Night Gets Out of Hand*

**Story Setup:**
Retta installs the bingo board. It's a Wednesday. She tells herself it'll be quiet: just
Darlene, Roy, and a few of their friends. By 7 PM there are eleven people crammed in the
backroom, someone has brought a casserole, Darlene is running the calls like a professional,
and a man named **Cletus Boone** has won three times and is starting to celebrate loudly
enough that the dryers can hear him. It is the best Wednesday of Retta's entire life.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Bingo mini-game:** Tap number tiles as they're called to mark the board. Players in the
backroom auto-participate. More players = more pot = more income, but also more noise = more
heat. A **Prize Pot mechanic** introduces high-risk/high-reward bingo rounds. A **Noise
Meter** (separate from Heat but feeding into it) appears during bingo sessions.

**New Room:**
**Backroom Expansion Phase 1:** Wall pushed back 4 tiles east. New layout has:
- Bingo board (wall-mounted)
- 4× Folding tables (bingo layout)
- 12× Chairs
- Small snack bar (popcorn machine + drink cooler)
- Darlene's "Official Bingo Caller Station" (a wooden stool with a microphone sticker on it)

**New NPC:**
**Cletus Boone** — retired hog farmer, surprisingly lucky, voice like a foghorn. He won the
first-ever bingo jackpot and now considers the backroom his personal kingdom. He arrives
early and leaves last. Risk level: High (he celebrates too loudly). Money potential: High.

**Conflict:**
The town deputy, **Clem Biggs**, is in the laundromat doing his laundry when the bingo
jackpot goes off and Cletus screams *"BINGO!"* so loud that the front-room customers all
look up. Clem pauses mid-fold. He heard something. He's not sure what. He wanders toward
the storage room door.

**Funny Event:**
*"Clem at the Door"* — Clem knocks on the storage room door (not the secret knock). Player choices:
- A: Open the door. Show Clem the bingo session. Say it's a charity night. (+Heat 15, but
  Clem decides it's fine and leaves, -Heat 10 next day)
- B: Don't answer. Put a handwritten "Asbestos — Do Not Enter" sign on the door in 4 seconds.
  (+Heat 5, Clem calls the county, mild chaos)
- C: Retta intercepts Clem before he reaches the door. Grandma Stare. Offers him a
  complimentary dryer sheet. Redirects him to a malfunctioning machine to investigate.
  (–Heat 5, reputation +5)

**Chapter Goal:**
- Host 3 bingo nights.
- Get 8+ players in a single bingo session.
- Earn $1,000 from backroom in this chapter.
- Survive the Clem encounter.

**Reward:**
- Unlock **Slot Machine Blueprint** (available next chapter).
- Upgrade the alley entrance with a better hidden door (reduces Heat from foot traffic).
- New Retta ability: **Emergency Bingo Cover** (instantly flips the backroom into "charity
  bingo night" mode for 3 minutes, reducing heat and giving cover for inspector/deputy visits).
- Cletus Boone becomes a Tier 2 backroom regular.

## CHAPTER 6 — "Hot Gossip"
### *The Town Starts Wondering*

**Story Setup:**
Connie Dupree has noticed things. Cars in the alley. Retta coming in before dawn to recount
money. A bag of poker chips she spotted through the window one time. She's put it all in the
church bulletin under "Prayer Requests" in a thinly veiled format. Half of Peavine has now
read the bulletin. The other half had it read to them by Connie personally. Deputy Clem has
started driving by twice a day. Health Inspector **Gerald Finch** is scheduled for his
annual visit. And Wade Pruett is watching all of this with great personal delight.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Heat system in full effect.** Heat events trigger at 25, 50, and 75 (see Heat System doc).
**Gossip Spread mechanic:** Connie has her own sub-meter that ticks up based on unresolved
rumors. **Gerald Finch visits** are a timed challenge: clean the front laundromat *and* hide
the backroom in a 90-second window before he arrives.

**New Room:**
**Fake wall installation:** A proper fake bookshelf/wall section is installed between the
storage room and the backroom. The secret door now requires a correct panel combination.
Reduces Heat-from-discovery by 25 points when triggered.

**New NPC:**
**Gerald Finch** — the health inspector. Anxious, methodical, clipboard-obsessed. He's not
trying to catch anyone; he just really loves compliance. He will find every actual code
violation (mopped floors, lint traps, detergent shelf labels) and use them as justification
to return more often. He is more annoying than threatening.

**Conflict:**
Gerald's inspection happens during a bingo session. The slot machine arrives that same
afternoon (delivery man knocks on the *wrong* door). Heat jumps to 40 for the first time.

**Funny Event:**
*"The Delivery Mix-Up"* — The slot machine is delivered to the front laundromat entrance
by mistake. It's labeled "Video Game System — Fragile." A customer asks if it's for the
waiting area. Player choices:
- A: Agree, roll it to the corner, play it off as entertainment. (Slot machine visible in
  front, +Heat 20, customers love it, must move it later)
- B: Rush to sign for it and push it out back immediately. Retta throws a laundry bag over it.
  (–Heat, +$50 handling chaos, good outcome but awkward dialogue)
- C: Tell the delivery man he has the wrong address and refuse delivery. Reschedule.
  (+2-day delay on slot machine, but Heat stays low)

**Chapter Goal:**
- Install the fake wall / secret door.
- Survive Gerald's inspection with ≥ 3 violations fixed.
- Keep Heat below 50 while keeping backroom revenue above $2,000 for the chapter.
- Reduce Connie's Gossip Meter below 30 (by giving her laundry discounts and misdirecting her).

**Reward:**
- Unlock **Slot Machine** (backroom).
- Gerald gives the laundromat a "Mostly Acceptable" rating. Laundromat reputation +15.
- Unlock **Reputation Bar #2 — Backroom Reputation** (the regulars are telling their friends).
- New Retta ability: **Coupon Confusion** (buries any NPC in discount paperwork for 45 seconds).

## CHAPTER 7 — "Competition Across the Street"
### *Wade Opens His Rival Laundromat*

**Story Setup:**
Wade Pruett opens **Pruett's Pristine Wash** three blocks away. It has new machines, a
digital payment kiosk, and a large sign that says "THE CLEAN CHOICE FOR PEAVINE." Wade also
runs a half-page ad in the Peavine Courier. Retta reads it over her morning coffee. She
underlines the phrase "your premier laundry experience" and tapes it to the wall behind the
counter where she can see it from her stool. She calls it her "motivational poster."

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Rival pressure mechanic:** Wade's laundromat steals a percentage of new customers each
week (configurable by competition level). Player must run upgrade campaigns and events to
win back market share. **Customer satisfaction bonuses** from atmosphere upgrades now matter
more. Special **"Beat Wade's Score"** weekly challenges appear.

**New Room:**
**Waiting Lounge expansion:** Full lounge area with comfortable seating (4-tile space):
- 4× Cushioned bench
- 1× Coffee station
- 1× Rack of magazines (decorative but boosts patience by 15%)
- 1× Wall TV showing a local weather channel on a loop
- 1× Restocked candy machine (upgraded vending)

**New NPC:**
**Wade Pruett** — pompous, self-satisfied, genuinely not as smart as he thinks he is. He
visits Retta's laundromat once a week to "keep an eye on the competition." He always asks
suspicious questions while pretending not to care. He is also Connie's cousin, which means
his information reaches her immediately.

**Conflict:**
Three of Retta's best regular customers switch to Pruett's for a week because of the new
machines. The backroom still brings in good money, but the front room income drops 20%.
Retta has to upgrade faster than planned and might need to let one staff member go temporarily.

**Funny Event:**
*"Wade's Visit"* — Wade comes in for his weekly "competitive analysis" (he's doing laundry,
he has no shame). He notices the new lounge, the better floor tiles, and the unusually heavy
foot traffic through the back. He says, *"You know, Loretta, for a little place, you sure
have a lot of… activity."* Player choices:
- A: Say nothing and smile. Offer him a free dryer sheet. (Best outcome, confuses him)
- B: Show him the ledger — the one with only laundromat income. (*"Clean as a whistle,
  Wade."*) Reputation +5, Wade suspicious but has no evidence.
- C: Tell him the backroom is a "wellness suite." He will tell Connie. +Heat 8.

**Chapter Goal:**
- Win back all 3 lost regular customers.
- Upgrade at least 2 front laundromat stations to Level 3.
- Keep front revenue within 10% of last chapter's total despite competition.
- Complete the lounge expansion.

**Reward:**
- Unlock **Premium Washer** (Level 4 tier, silver-chrome visual).
- Wade's competitive pressure mechanic scales down (Retta is firmly the local favorite).
- Unlock **Backroom Expansion Phase 2** blueprint (VIP corner + poker table).
- Retta ability upgrade: **Grandma Stare** now works on Wade specifically (sends him away
  without conversation for 10 minutes, once per day).

## CHAPTER 8 — "The Expansion"
### *Backroom Gets Serious*

**Story Setup:**
The backroom is running out of room. Cletus Boone has been waiting forty-five minutes for a
bingo seat. Roy Macabee showed up with a friend who showed up with two friends. There are
currently 17 people in a room rated for 8, someone's elbow is in the popcorn machine, and
Darlene is trying to call bingo numbers over what sounds like a debate about horse racing.
The wall needs to come down. The real expansion begins.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Backroom management system expands:** Poker table available with multiplayer rounds.
**VIP Corner** for high rollers (Roy, Cletus, new high-roller NPCs) generates 3× base income
but +15 Heat per session. **Staff backroom roles** are now available to hire. **Lookout
camera** system upgraded: 3-second warning before suspicious NPCs arrive.

**New Room:**
**Backroom Expansion Phase 2** — Full double-width backroom (12×10 tiles):
- 1× Bingo board (upgraded, wall-mounted with scoreboard)
- 2× Slot machines
- 1× Poker table (6 seats)
- 1× VIP Corner (velvet rope, 2 tables, mood lighting)
- 1× Lucky Wheel
- 1× Proper snack bar (Darlene mans it)
- 1× Dedicated "staff only" corner with radio and emergency button
- 1× Second alley exit (escape hallway, Phase 1)

**New NPC:**
**Minnie "High Roller" Vashti** — A retired math teacher from Lubbock who moved to Peavine
to be near her grandkids. She plays poker like she grades essays: methodically and without
mercy. She is polite, composed, and has won more money from Roy Macabee than anyone will
ever admit. Risk: Medium. Value: Very High.

**Conflict:**
The expansion requires a contractor. The contractor Retta hires is the brother of Deputy
Clem. Clem starts asking his brother innocent questions about "what you're building back
there." The brother says *"some kind of entertainment room"* and Clem drives by six times
in two days.

**Funny Event:**
*"The Lucky Wheel Incident"* — During the lucky wheel's first spin, it malfunctions and
starts playing music at full volume. Not just any music — a loud brass-band version of a
familiar folk song. The whole front laundromat hears it. Customers start clapping.
Player choices:
- A: Embrace it. Tell front customers there's a "special event." Heat +10 but reputation +15.
- B: Denny the repair guy dives on the wheel. 30-second frantic repair mini-game.
- C: Retta power-walks to the backroom, unplugs the wheel, and says *"We'll fix that,
  praise the Lord."* to anyone who looks her way. Clean outcome, funny dialogue.

**Chapter Goal:**
- Complete the Phase 2 backroom expansion.
- Host 5 poker nights.
- Earn $5,000 from the backroom this chapter.
- Install the escape hallway.

**Reward:**
- Unlock **VIP Corner** (full tier).
- Minnie Vashti becomes a Tier 3 backroom regular.
- Retta ability unlocked: **Family Emergency** (sends any single NPC away from the building
  for a full in-game day with one believable story).
- **The Lint Trap** — Retta names the backroom. It now has a sign. On the *inside* of the door.

## CHAPTER 9 — "Heat Gets Serious"
### *Close Calls and Hard Choices*

**Story Setup:**
The Peavine Courier has run a small item: *"Anonymous source reports unusual activity near
Gipson Street."* Connie Dupree has sent three prayer requests to the church bulletin about
businesses that "lead the community astray." Gerald Finch is doing a *second* inspection
this year — unprecedented. Clem Biggs has started showing up in plainclothes, which is a
much worse sign than showing up in uniform because at least the uniform gives you warning.
Retta has kept the front laundromat spotless. She has also won three poker hands this month
while technically running the register. It is fine. Everything is fine.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Multiple simultaneous threats.** Heat events can now stack: two different sources raising
heat at once. **Negotiation events** with Clem give the player moral-choice outcomes (bribe
with pie, distract with broken machine, call his bluff). **Connie's gossip meter** enters
a red zone — active gossip-quashing mini-quests appear. **Gerald's inspection** is now a
timed, multi-room audit.

**New Room:**
**Soundproofing upgrade** (backroom): full soundproofing installed. Reduces ambient Heat
gain from backroom by 40%. Visually, foam tiles appear on the backroom walls. Also unlocks:
**Fake Storage Room** corridor — a narrow room between the back exit and the alley that
contains actual laundry supplies, making it look legitimate if anyone opens the door.

**New NPC:**
**Deputy Clem Biggs (Advanced State)** — Clem in plainclothes is Clem at his most dangerous
and also his most confused. He genuinely cannot tell if Retta is running something illegal or
if she just has very popular bingo nights. He is not bad-hearted. He's just doing his job
very slowly. He can be befriended: if reputation is high enough, Clem becomes a soft ally
who warns Retta of incoming inspections.

**Conflict:**
Three converging threats in one chapter: Connie gossip at 80, Gerald Finch inspection
scheduled, Clem in plainclothes has been sitting in a rocking chair across the street for
two afternoons in a row. Any single one of these is manageable. All three at once is the
biggest test of the game so far.

**Funny Event:**
*"The Perfect Storm"* — Gerald Finch arrives early. Clem is already inside doing a "normal"
load of laundry. Connie is at the vending machine. Three regulars are in the backroom and
the poker table is mid-hand. Player must:
1. Use **Coupon Confusion** on Gerald (buy 45 seconds).
2. Assign the floor manager to distract Clem with a complicated machine issue.
3. Tap the emergency button to lock the backroom.
4. Have Retta reach Connie within 10 seconds and offer her a bingo discount card.
If all four succeed: the scene ends in comedy. If any fails: a cascading chain of events
begins that can raise Heat to 75.

**Chapter Goal:**
- Survive all three threats.
- Install soundproofing.
- Build the fake storage room.
- Keep Heat below 75 while earning $7,500 in backroom revenue this chapter.

**Reward:**
- Heat decay rate permanently increased by 15% (Retta is getting better at this).
- **Deputy Clem** friendship meter unlocks: befriend him for protection, or keep him at
  arm's length for drama.
- Unlock Chapter 10 finale.
- Retta ability upgrade: **Clean Sweep** now also locks all backroom entrances automatically.

## CHAPTER 10 — "The Peavine Jubilee"
### *The Town Festival & Big Finale*

**Story Setup:**
Every year, Peavine holds its Summer Jubilee: a street fair, a pie contest, and an outdoor
concert on the town square. This year, the Jubilee is happening right outside Retta's
laundromat. There will be 500% more foot traffic than usual. Connie Dupree is on the
Jubilee committee. Gerald Finch is running the food safety booth. Deputy Clem is running the
parking situation. And the Peavine Courier is there with a camera.

Retta's front laundromat is running a "Jubilee Special" discount. The backroom has been
quietly sold out for three weeks in advance. If everything goes perfectly — and it will not
go perfectly — it will be the single greatest day of income in the building's history.
And then something will go wrong. Something always goes wrong. Retta would have it no other way.

**New Gameplay Feature:**
**Festival mode:** Customer spawn rate is 3× normal. A new "overflow" section of the
laundromat opens (temporary Jubilee tent attached to the outside). The backroom runs at
maximum capacity. All Heat events from the entire game can appear in random order during
the Jubilee day. **Boss event:** *The Jubilee Crisis* (see below).

**New Room:**
**Jubilee tent exterior** (temporary):
- 2× Extra washers (pop-up, lower quality)
- Snack cart
- "Laundry Contest" table (silly community event: who can fold a sheet fastest — free
  marketing for the laundromat)

**New NPC:**
**The Peavine Courier Reporter** — **Barry Nell**, a nervous young journalist looking for
his big story. He keeps trying to interview Retta. He asks questions that could be read
two ways. Retta gives him answers that can only be read one way. He's frustrated. He's also
starting to enjoy her company.

**Conflict (Boss Event — "The Jubilee Crisis"):**
At 6 PM during the Jubilee, five things happen at once:
1. A water pipe bursts in the laundromat (back near the storage room wall).
2. Cletus Boone wins the jackpot and his victory whoop can be heard two blocks away.
3. Gerald Finch is talking to Clem right outside the front door.
4. Connie Dupree is live-streaming the Jubilee on her phone and has walked into the laundromat.
5. Wade Pruett's laundromat sign across the street falls on his car and he screams.

Player must navigate all five using abilities, staff, and decision events in sequence.
The outcomes — good or bad — determine the **Finale Ending**.

**Three Finale Endings:**

**Ending A — "Clean Record"** (Heat below 30 at finale):
Retta comes through the Jubilee spotless. Barry Nell writes a glowing article about
"Peavine's favorite new business." Clem eats pie. Gerald gives Retta an "Exemplary
Establishment" certificate. The backroom continues undetected, now called "The Lint Trap"
in whispered reverence by its regulars. Roy cries a little. Darlene bakes a cake. Credits roll.

**Ending B — "Famous"** (Heat 31–70 at finale):
The Jubilee story gets out — sort of. Barry Nell writes a wildly inaccurate article titled
*"Local Grandma Runs 'Community Entertainment Venue.'"* Nobody is quite sure what it means.
The backroom becomes an open secret. Clem pretends not to know. Gerald visits one more time
and sees a bingo board. He is confused for weeks. The laundromat triples in business.

**Ending C — "The Legend"** (Heat 71–99 at finale, survived):
Everything comes out. Sort of. Connie's livestream goes mildly viral in the county.
Barry writes a feature. Gerald and Clem are in it. Wade Pruett is furious and demands justice.
But nobody can prove anything specifically illegal about a bingo night and a poker club among
friends. Retta is summoned to a town council meeting. She brings sweet tea and pie. She gives
a 7-minute speech that ends with, *"I have never in my life done anything I was ashamed of.
And I am not about to start now."* The room applauds. The council votes 4-1 to issue her a
"Community Entertainment" license. The one dissenter is Wade Pruett.

**Chapter Goal:**
- Serve 200 customers during the Jubilee.
- Survive the Jubilee Crisis.
- Earn $15,000 total revenue in this chapter.
