# Section 23: First 30 Minutes — Player Experience

A beat-by-beat walkthrough of the player's first session. Designed to introduce every
core mechanic naturally, establish Retta's voice and the game's tone, and end with the
player curious about the storage room.

Total estimated first-session time: ~25–30 minutes of active play.

---

## MINUTE 0:00 — TITLE SCREEN

**What the player sees:**
Full-screen pixel art of a small Texas laundromat at night. The neon sign reads
"Old Dewey's Wash-O-Rama" — but the "O" is flickering off and on.
A silhouette of a small woman in an apron is visible through the window.

**Music:** "Gipson Street" begins softly.

**Text:** *"WASH & WAGER"* appears in the slab-serif title font, warm amber glow.
Subline: *"A Retta Hollis Story"*

**Tap to Begin →**

---

## MINUTE 0:15 — OPENING CUTSCENE

**4 dialogue panels** (tap to advance, bottom-screen dialogue format):

**Panel 1:**
[Retta stands outside a rundown laundromat, arms crossed, appraising it.]
*Retta: "Well. It's seen better days."*

**Panel 2:**
[Earl Jr. stands beside her, holding a clipboard and clearly regretting something.]
*Earl Jr.: "Retta, you paid what— for THIS? The sign is half dark. There are only two
working machines. One of the dryers smells like a— I don't want to say what it smells like."*

**Panel 3:**
[Retta turns and pats Earl Jr.'s arm with a serene smile.]
*Retta: "Earl, honey. Your grandfather built a living room out of a chicken coop.
I am going to build a business out of a laundromat."*

**Panel 4:**
[Wide shot: Retta pushes the front door open. Light spills out.]
*Retta: "Now come help me fix that washer before the first customer gets here."*

---

## MINUTE 1:00 — TUTORIAL BEGINS (Laundromat View Unlocks)

**Player sees the starting map** for the first time. Top-down, cozy front laundromat.
Two washers have wrench icons floating above them. The coin machine has a jam icon.
The front entrance door is open.

**Tutorial Step 1: Repair the first washer**
- A subtle pulsing arrow points to Washer 1.
- Tooltip: *"TAP Washer 1 to repair it."*
- Player taps → Retta walks to the machine → 5-second repair animation → machine lights up → wrench disappears.
- **Sound:** repair sounds → completion chime.
- **Voice line above Retta:** *"Don't make me ask twice."*

**Tutorial Step 2: Repair the second washer**
- No arrow this time — player is expected to remember.
- Tooltip: *"Washer 2 needs the same treatment."*
- Repeat.
- Small coin bag floats up after second repair (reward: $10).
- Tutorial: *"TAP the coin bag to collect it."*
- Player taps coin bag → Retta reaches out → coins fly into the money counter.
- **Sound:** Bright *ching!*

**Tutorial Step 3: Fix the coin machine**
- Tooltip: *"The coin machine is jammed. Customers need it to get quarters."*
- Tap machine → quick animation → unjam sound → machine glows.

---

## MINUTE 3:00 — FIRST CUSTOMER

**Sound:** Door chime as the first customer enters.

**Character:** A **Tired Mom** (Sandra) with two laundry bags and a toddler on her hip.
She walks to the coin machine, exchanges a bill, walks to Washer 1, loads clothes, starts the cycle.

**Tutorial Step 4: Watch the customer flow**
- Tooltip fades in: *"Customers do their own laundry. Your job: keep the machines running
  and the floor clean."*
- A gentle timer bar above the washer shows the cycle in progress.
- A small coin icon appears above the washer when the cycle completes (the customer pays).

**Player sees for the first time:** A small spill appears near the folding tables
(the mom's bag dripped). A mop bucket icon pulses.

**Tutorial Step 5: Mop the spill**
- Tooltip: *"Spills make customers unhappy. Tap the puddle to mop it up."*
- Player taps puddle → Retta mops → sparkle → Wanda note unlocked in the side panel
  (suggestion bubble: *"Hire a cleaner so you don't have to do this yourself?"*).

---

## MINUTE 5:00 — FIRST INCOME

The first washer cycle completes. A coin bag pops above it.

- **Tutorial Step 6: Collect income**
- Tooltip: *"The cycle's done. Collect your coins!"*
- Player taps → $1.50 collected → money counter ticks up.
- Retta voice line: *"That's what I thought."*

A second customer enters — a **College Kid** (Tyler) — slightly confused at the coin machine.
The first customer has moved to the dryer. The folding table is next.

**Player is now managing 2 customer flows simultaneously without instruction.**
This is intentional — the rhythm clicks naturally.

---

## MINUTE 7:00 — FIRST UPGRADE PROMPT

The Upgrade menu flashes softly (not aggressively — a gentle tap animation on the top bar icon).

**Tutorial Step 7: Open the Upgrade Menu**
- Tooltip: *"You have enough to upgrade your detergent shelf. More products = more sales."*
- Player opens the upgrade menu, sees the detergent shelf at L1 with the upgrade button priced at $200.
- Current coins: ~$250 (from first few cycles + starting funds).
- Player can afford it. Tap to upgrade.
- **Animation:** Shelf tiles update (new bottles appear). Upgrade chime plays.
- Tooltip: *"Customers can now buy fabric softener. Small upgrade, real income."*

---

## MINUTE 9:00 — FIRST PROBLEM (MACHINE BREAKDOWN)

Washer 1 breaks down mid-cycle. Customer waiting for it starts losing patience (meter ticks).

- **Tutorial Step 8: React to a breakdown**
- Tooltip: *"Washer 1 just broke down. Tap it quickly before the customer leaves!"*
- No timer pressure on the tooltip — but the customer patience bar is visibly ticking.
- Player taps → Retta repairs → machine restarts → customer's patience refills slightly.
- Retta voice line: *"Three taps. Works every time."*

If the player is slow (>20 sec): customer leaves with an angry expression.
Tooltip: *"Some customers won't wait. Faster repairs = happier customers."*
Then Retta voice line: *"Well. Can't win 'em all."* (Tone is philosophical, not grim.)

---

## MINUTE 11:00 — STAFF HINT: HIRE WANDA

5 spills have occurred by now. Wanda's hire suggestion appears in the task queue.

- **Tutorial Step 9: Open the Staff Menu**
- Tooltip: *"Mopping everything yourself gets old fast. Wanda Pickle is available to hire."*
- Staff menu shows Wanda at $150 hire cost, $80/week.
- Current money: ~$400. Player can afford it.
- Hire Wanda → Wanda sprite appears in the building → immediately starts cleaning a spill.
- Sound: *ding-dong* hire sound.
- **Wanda's first dialogue:** *"Finally. My floor now."*
- Tooltip: *"Staff handles tasks automatically. You stay focused on the big picture."*

---

## MINUTE 14:00 — FIRST STORY DIALOGUE (ROY)

**Roy Macabee** enters for the first time. He's a small, quiet sprite. He goes to the coin machine.
He goes to Washer 2. He starts his cycle. He waits on the bench. He reads nothing. He just sits.

After 2 minutes of play-time, when the player interacts near the back of the building
(cleaning the bathroom or near the storage room):

**Cutscene trigger:**
[Roy materializes beside Retta with uncanny quiet. She startles slightly — a rare event.]

*Roy: "Good machines. Dewey never kept the machines this clean."*
*Retta: "You knew Dewey?"*
*Roy: "Used to come here a fair amount. Back in the day."*

[Roy's eyes drift to the storage room door. One beat.]

*Roy: "Good to have this place open again."*

He walks back to his bench. His laundry is done. He collects it. He tips $2.
He leaves. The storage room door has a very faint soft glow effect — almost nothing.
Just a flicker.

---

## MINUTE 17:00 — SECOND MACHINE UPGRADE

Enough income has accumulated for the first real machine upgrade ($500 for Washer L2).

- **Tutorial Step 10: First machine upgrade**
- Gentle prompt (not forced): *"Your washers could be faster. Check the upgrade menu."*
- Player upgrades Washer 1 to L2.
- Visual change: machine tile updates to lighter color, new decal.
- Effect: cycle time drops from 3 min to 2.5 min.
- Three new customers arrive simultaneously (reward for upgrade — increased traffic).
- Money counter is visibly ticking faster.

---

## MINUTE 20:00 — VENDING MACHINE COMES ONLINE

The vending machine slot has been available since Chapter 1's completion reward.
If not yet purchased, a gentle prompt appears:

*"The vending machine slot is ready. Customers are already looking at it."*
*"Cost: $400"*

If the player purchases it: vending machine installs → first customer immediately buys a snack.
Daily passive income display appears in the upgrade menu.

---

## MINUTE 22:00 — CHAPTER 1 GOALS NEARLY MET

**Chapter Status Panel (slide-up from the Story icon):**

```
CHAPTER 1 — The Rusty Drum
✅ Fix both broken washers
✅ Fix the coin machine
Progress: 18/20 customers served
Progress: $380 / $500 revenue
```

The player can see they're nearly at the goal. Income is flowing. The floor is clean.
Two machines are upgraded.

**Retta voice line (ambient):** *"Getting there."*

---

## MINUTE 24:00 — FIRST STORY EVENT (DEWEY'S MANUAL)

**The Handoff event triggers.** (See Event doc, Event 01 reference in Chapter 1 description.)

Dewey materializes at the front door as a brief NPC (he's never in the building again after this).
He has a handwritten manual. He gives it to Retta. She thanks him. He says:
*"Oh, and the storage room in back — door sticks. Don't open it if you got company."*

*Retta: "And why's that?"*

*Dewey pauses. He nods slowly. He tips his hat. He leaves.*

Retta looks at the storage room door. She looks back at the manual.
She tucks it in her apron.

**Player note in the margin of Dewey's manual** (visible in the Story menu):
*"Storage room. Investigate."*

---

The last two customers complete their cycles. Money counter crosses $500.

**Chapter Complete Screen:**
- Chapter 1 — The Rusty Drum ✅
- Revenue: ~$520
- Customers served: 21
- Heat: 0
- **Reward:** $200 + Detergent shelf (already bought) + Vending machine slot + Bless Your Heart ability

**Retta voice line on the chapter complete screen:**
*"Not bad, Retta. Not bad at all."*

## MINUTE 29:00 — CHAPTER 2 STARTS

The new chapter begins with a brief cutscene:

*Retta stands behind her counter. The sign outside now reads "WASH & WAGER"
(the neon has been fixed — Earl Jr. did it, silently, without being asked).*

*More customers are visible through the window. The vending machine glows.*

*Retta: "All right. Day two. Let's see what Peavine's made of."*

**New element appears:** The Vending Machine is now active and restocking becomes a
new task in the queue. Two more customers arrive simultaneously.

**Connie Dupree enters for the first time** — she moves slowly, heads to a machine,
does one load. She spends most of her time walking around the perimeter of the building.
Her Gossip Meter begins silently at 0.

---

## FIRST 30 MINUTES — SUMMARY

By minute 30, the player has experienced:
- ✅ The full laundromat loop (customers → machines → collect)
- ✅ Machine repair and mopping
- ✅ Their first staff hire (Wanda)
- ✅ Their first upgrade (detergent shelf + Washer L2)
- ✅ The coin collection rhythm
- ✅ Retta's personality and voice
- ✅ Roy Macabee's introduction
- ✅ Dewey's storage room hint
- ✅ Connie's introduction (without the player yet knowing she's a rival)
- ✅ Chapter 1 complete + Chapter 2 beginning

**Not yet experienced (intentionally held back):**
- The storage room (Chapter 4 — builds anticipation)
- The Heat Meter (not yet relevant)
- The backroom (Chapter 4)
- Staff beyond Wanda (budget-permitting; some players hire Denny next)
- Wade Pruett (Chapter 3+ encounter)

The first 30 minutes establish: *"This is a satisfying laundromat game."*
The rest of the game establishes: *"...and it's also so much more."*
