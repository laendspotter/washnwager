# Sections 7–8: Customer Types

---

## Section 7: Normal Laundromat Customers (22 Types)

All normal customers self-serve. They arrive, use machines, and leave. Retta's job is
to keep the machines running, the place clean, and the customers patient enough not to leave.

**Patience Level:** ★ (leaves in 30s) to ★★★★★ (waits 5+ min)
**Spending Level:** $ (min spend) to $$$$$ (big spender)

---

### 1. The College Kid
**Name archetype:** Tyler / Skylar / Becca
**Behavior:** Arrives with exactly one garbage bag crammed full of laundry he's been
saving for three weeks. Always short on quarters. Checks his phone 400 times during the cycle.
**Patience:** ★★★ (tolerant, has nowhere better to be)
**Spending:** $ (absolute minimum; will try to use too little detergent)
**Funny complaint:** *"This machine ate my dollar AGAIN and I have to Venmo my roommate
for this load and my phone is at 4%."*
**Possible event:** Finds another student's lost homework in the dryer. Causes mild drama.

---

### 2. The Tired Mom
**Name archetype:** Michelle / Sandra / Keisha
**Behavior:** Arrives with two kids, a stroller, a diaper bag, and four laundry bags.
She moves with the practiced efficiency of someone who has not slept since 2019. She will
fold clothes while standing in line, while holding a baby, while on the phone.
**Patience:** ★★★★ (she has waited longer for worse things)
**Spending:** $$$ (buys all the things — detergent, dryer sheets, snacks for the kids)
**Funny complaint:** *"Don't touch that machine, Jackson. Don't you DARE, Jackson. JACKSON."*
**Possible event:** One of her kids accidentally starts a different customer's washer cycle.

---

### 3. The Construction Worker
**Name archetype:** Hank / Dale / Reggie
**Behavior:** Comes in his work boots, leaves boot-print scuff marks on the floor. His
laundry smells like an adventure. He means well and tips surprisingly well if the machines
are running.
**Patience:** ★★★ (used to waiting on job sites)
**Spending:** $$ (simple needs, no frills)
**Funny complaint:** *"This dryer's weaker than my ex-wife's handshake. You got one in
the back that actually spins?"*
**Possible event:** Drops a fistful of loose screws into Washer 2. Jamming event triggered.

---

### 4. The Fancy Lady
**Name archetype:** Vivienne / Margot / Patricia
**Behavior:** Only here because her home washing machine is being repaired. She is visibly
unhappy about it. She brings her own detergent (Tier 5), refuses to use the folding tables,
and holds her clothes at arm's length while inspecting them.
**Patience:** ★ (two minutes, then she's calling someone)
**Spending:** $$$$ (buys premium items, tips zero, but upgrades her cycle twice)
**Funny complaint:** *"This machine has water stains inside it. The machine that washes
things has water stains. Do you understand the irony?"*
**Possible event:** Accidentally runs her silk blouse in a hot cycle. The ensuing conversation
is a diplomatic crisis.

---

### 5. The Broke Teenager
**Name archetype:** Jaylen / Devon / Cassie
**Behavior:** Comes in with $3.50 and a half-empty bottle of dish soap. Makes it work
somehow. Sits on a dryer (against the rules). Has earbuds in the whole time.
**Patience:** ★★★★★ (infinite — he has no schedule)
**Spending:** $ (bare minimum)
**Funny complaint:** *"Can I use the bathroom? The door says 'Customers Only' and I'm
technically a customer."* (He spent $0.75. Retta decides.)
**Possible event:** Falls asleep on a bench. Has to be woken up at closing.

---

### 6. The Retired Veteran
**Name archetype:** Frank / Harold / Otis
**Behavior:** Arrives early. Folds his laundry into perfect geometric squares. Reads the
newspaper in the waiting area. Has opinions about how the machines should be arranged.
**Patience:** ★★★★★ (infinite; he's done harder things)
**Spending:** $$ (frugal but consistent; always pays exact change)
**Funny complaint:** *"That dryer's running 2 minutes slow. I timed it. Yesterday it was
3 minutes slow. Something's loose in the drum."* (He is correct.)
**Possible event:** Notices a screw missing from a machine panel and offers to fix it.
Give him a screwdriver and he will fix three other things too.

---

### 7. The One-Sock Guy
**Name archetype:** Steve / Gary / Norm
**Behavior:** Always comes in with a look on his face that says he just remembered something
important. Leaves one sock behind every single visit without fail. He doesn't notice until
he's three blocks away. Returns. The sock is always gone.
**Patience:** ★★★ (distracted patience)
**Spending:** $$
**Funny complaint:** *"I had a matching pair when I walked in. I have checked the machine,
the floor, the trash, and my own pockets. I would like to report a sock."*
**Possible event:** His lost sock turns up in another customer's laundry. Complex social situation.

---

### 8. The Gossip Neighbor
**Name archetype:** Barbara / Dolores / Janet
**Behavior:** Shows up weekly. Does one load. Stays for three hours. Has not missed a
piece of neighborhood drama in 15 years. She talks at the same volume as a lawn mower.
If Connie Dupree and Barbara are both in the building at once, the gossip output is
classified as a local noise event.
**Patience:** ★★★★★ (she's not leaving; this is her show)
**Spending:** $$ (regular, steady)
**Funny complaint:** *"I heard that noise from the back again. Is that a TV? It sounds
like someone celebrating. Who's celebrating? Why wasn't I told?"*
**Possible event:** Starts a rumor about the laundromat based on something she
half-overheard. Reputation -5. Heat +3.

---

### 9. The Impatient Businessman
**Name archetype:** Brad / Craig / Derek
**Behavior:** Drives up in a company car. Needs his dress shirts done in 30 minutes.
Checks his phone constantly. Uses the words "optimized," "synergy," and "honestly" in
every sentence. Cannot operate the coin machine.
**Patience:** ★ (gone in 90 seconds if machine isn't free)
**Spending:** $$$$$ (will pay extra for any speed upgrade, tips big if fast)
**Funny complaint:** *"So there's NO dry-clean service? And there's no app? And you're
telling me I have to use QUARTERS?"*
**Possible event:** Gets on a work call and starts talking loudly about "restructuring" while
other customers passive-aggressively cough.

---

### 10. The Chaotic Family
**Name archetype:** The Hendersons / The Garcias / The Watsons
**Behavior:** 2 adults, 4 kids, 6 laundry bags, a picnic blanket, a stuffed animal, and
a loose dog that technically wasn't supposed to come in. They use every machine simultaneously.
**Patience:** ★★ (the adults are patient; the kids are agents of entropy)
**Spending:** $$$$ (volume buyers; stock up on everything)
**Funny complaint:** *"Somebody let the dog into the dryer room. He's not in a dryer.
That's just where he ran."*
**Possible event:** One child climbs into a front-load washer. It is not running. It is fine.
But everyone has to stop and deal with it anyway. Heat generated: none. Chaos: maximum.

---

### 11. The Night Shift Worker
**Name archetype:** Latoya / Marcus / Deb
**Behavior:** Shows up at odd hours looking slightly sideways. Perfectly friendly. Just tired.
Does laundry on autopilot, sometimes puts the fabric softener in the detergent slot, doesn't notice.
**Patience:** ★★★★ (too tired to care about wait times)
**Spending:** $$ (steady, reliable)
**Funny complaint:** *"Did I put detergent in or did I just pour in my coffee? I honestly
cannot tell you."*
**Possible event:** Falls asleep on the bench and starts talking in her sleep. Customers
are fascinated.

---

### 12. The Aspiring YouTuber
**Name archetype:** Chase / Olivia / Bri
**Behavior:** Doing a "laundromat vlog." Asks if they can film Retta. Retta says yes, then
makes sure they film the front laundromat specifically and not any other rooms.
**Patience:** ★★★ (too busy filming to leave)
**Spending:** $$$ (buys dramatic story items for the vlog: a big bottle of detergent, snacks)
**Funny complaint:** *"The lighting in here is kinda bad but honestly it gives the content
that 'authentic gritty' vibe. Can I get you to say something pithy for the camera?"*
**Possible event:** Their video gets 4,000 views. Customer traffic +10% for one week.
But it also shows the alley door in the background. Heat +8.

---

### 13. The Dog Walker
**Name archetype:** Trish / Andy / Gwen
**Behavior:** Comes in with three dogs on leashes. The dogs are "outside," she insists,
while the leashes trail in through the propped door. She washes dog blankets exclusively.
**Patience:** ★★★ (patient for herself; dogs are not)
**Spending:** $$ (dog blankets use the delicate cycle; she buys premium detergent)
**Funny complaint:** *"Rosie doesn't like the dryer sound. She needs you to maybe make
it quieter? Just while she's here? She's very sensitive."*
**Possible event:** A dog gets loose and beelines for the backroom alley door. Retta
intercepts with remarkable speed.

---

### 14. The Newlywed
**Name archetype:** (couple names) Sam & Jamie / Trey & Destiny / Alex & Morgan
**Behavior:** Doing laundry together for the first time. Discover they have completely
different philosophies on sorting colors. The argument is polite but deeply felt.
**Patience:** ★★★★ (still in the honeymoon phase)
**Spending:** $$$ (buying new laundry supplies for their new place)
**Funny complaint (combined):** *"I separate lights and darks." / "We never did that growing
up." / "That explains SO MUCH."*
**Possible event:** They ask Retta for advice on laundry sorting. She gives them a 3-minute
speech that ends with commentary on marriage. They look thoughtful.

---

### 15. The Superstitious Regular
**Name archetype:** Consuelo / Bobby Gene / Edna
**Behavior:** Uses the exact same machine every single visit. Refuses to use Washer 3 because
"something ain't right about that one." Will wait 25 minutes for their lucky machine rather
than use a different one.
**Patience:** ★★★★★ (infinite — they're waiting for their machine specifically)
**Spending:** $$ (predictable, loyal)
**Funny complaint:** *"I don't care if Washer 2 is busy. I will wait. I have waited 40 minutes
before and I will wait 40 minutes again. That machine knows me."*
**Possible event:** Their lucky machine breaks. The resulting interaction is either very sweet
or extremely weird depending on how Retta handles it.

---

### 16. The Extreme Couponer
**Name archetype:** Linda / Phyllis / June
**Behavior:** Has a coupon for everything. Attempts to use a coupon that expired in 2018.
Disputes the price of dryer sheets. Asks for a manager (she is talking to the manager).
**Patience:** ★★★★ (will stay to argue a point)
**Spending:** $ (tries to spend nothing; actually spends $$ after negotiations)
**Funny complaint:** *"This says 'one load special' and I am technically loading everything
into one machine so that IS one load. That IS what that says."*
**Possible event:** Finds a promotion code from a 2019 flyer and demands it be honored.
Player choice: honor it (-$2, reputation +10) or deny it (-reputation, funny argument).

---

### 17. The Nurse/Doctor
**Name archetype:** Dr. Keene / Nurse Paige / Dr. Morris
**Behavior:** Comes in wearing scrubs, clearly just off a long shift. Polite, efficient,
absolutely no nonsense. When the coin machine jams she fixes it herself in 45 seconds.
**Patience:** ★★★★ (trained to wait; trained to notice things)
**Spending:** $$$ (buys everything in one efficient pass)
**Funny complaint:** *"That machine's making a sound that suggests a loose bearing on the
drum motor. I'd fix it before Tuesday or you'll lose the whole drum."* (She is correct.)
**Possible event:** Notices someone having a minor medical issue in the waiting area and
handles it calmly. Reputation +15.

---

### 18. The High School Coach
**Name archetype:** Coach Dwayne / Coach Brandy / Coach Garrett
**Behavior:** Comes in with nine bags of football/volleyball/track gear. Loudly motivates
the washing machines. Gives the dryers pep talks.
**Patience:** ★★★★ (motivational energy)
**Spending:** $$$ (volume + sports detergent)
**Funny complaint:** *"This machine is SLOW. This machine is not giving 100%. I need 100%
from this machine RIGHT NOW."*
**Possible event:** Recruits a bored customer into an impromptu laundry-folding race. The
customer wins. Coach is disproportionately delighted.

---

### 19. The True Crime Podcast Person
**Name archetype:** Amber / Jake / Savannah
**Behavior:** Headphones in, notepad out. Treating the laundromat like a research location
for their podcast. Keeps writing things down and glancing around.
**Patience:** ★★★★ (note-taking keeps them busy)
**Spending:** $$ (basic)
**Funny complaint (to her microphone, not to Retta):** *"Sources confirm unusual foot traffic
through the rear of the establishment. The owner appears to have a detailed awareness of
every person's location at all times. Could be experience. Could be something else entirely."*
**Possible event:** She tries to interview Retta for the podcast. Retta says yes and talks
for 20 minutes about the history of fabric softener in the American South. The episode airs.
Heat +5 from the attention, but also reputation +20.

---

### 20. The Preacher's Wife
**Name archetype:** Elaine Tharp / Martha / Darlene Lee
**Behavior:** Sweet, chatty, invites everyone to Sunday service. Notices details. Specifically
notices that Roy Macabee takes a very long time in the back area and doesn't appear to be
carrying laundry.
**Patience:** ★★★★★ (church training)
**Spending:** $$$ (large family's laundry, regular customer)
**Funny complaint:** *"I'm not one to say anything, Loretta, but there were some very loud
celebrations from back there and I do hope everyone is all right. It sounded like someone
was very happy about something."*
**Possible event:** Invites Retta to donate to the church charity raffle. Player choice:
donate ($) for reputation, refuse (neutral), or offer to run a "special charity bingo night"
(Heat +5, reputation +25).

---

### 21. The Paranoid Neighbor
**Name archetype:** Ralph / Mervin / Glen
**Behavior:** Lives two doors down. Convinced the laundromat is running something sketchy.
Comes in weekly to do laundry and gather evidence. Has no actual evidence. Writes letters
to the city council anyway.
**Patience:** ★★ (impatient; he has a conspiracy to run)
**Spending:** $
**Funny complaint:** *"Why do people come in through the alley? Normal businesses don't
have alley traffic. I've been counting. Tuesday, seven people. Wednesday, eleven."*
**Possible event:** He actually finds something (a poker chip, a bingo card). Player must
defuse him before he goes to Clem. Time-limited event.

---

### 22. The Snowbird Retiree
**Name archetype:** Herb / Shirley / Mabel
**Behavior:** Only here for three months from up north. Absolutely enchanted by Peavine.
Delighted by everything. Asks if the coin machine "is one of those new digital things."
**Patience:** ★★★★★ (retired; nowhere to be)
**Spending:** $$$$ (generous; tips like a tourist)
**Funny complaint:** *"Back home these machines are $3.50 a load. This is practically FREE.
Does the owner know? Someone should tell her she's undercharging."* (Player can raise prices.)
**Possible event:** Tells everyone at the senior center about Retta's laundromat. Surge of
first-time elderly customers that week. All have infinite patience and very large laundry bags.

## Section 8: Backroom Customer Types (17 Types)

Backroom regulars arrive via the alley. They know about The Lint Trap through word of mouth.
Their visit frequency, behavior, and money contribution vary by tier.

**Risk Level:** 🟢 Low / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 High
**Money Potential:** $ to $$$$$

---

### 1. Roy Macabee — "The Faithful Regular"
**Behavior:** Shows up every Tuesday without fail. Plays cards with quiet focus. Never wins
big, never loses big. Has been coming since before Retta opened it up properly.
**Risk:** 🟢 Low (discreet, loyal, never causes problems)
**Money:** $$ (consistent but small)
**Dialogue:** *"Evening, Retta. The usual, please. And I'll take a sweet tea if you got one."*
**Special event:** "Roy's Big Night" — once per chapter, Roy has an unusually good run.
He wins $200 and nearly cries. He tips Retta $20 and says it's the best night he's had
in years. Reputation +10 in the backroom.

---

### 2. Darlene Hooks — "The Bingo Queen"
**Behavior:** Only comes for bingo nights. She is the unofficial bingo queen of Peavine County.
She runs her own dauber, her own lucky charm (a small ceramic rooster), and her own score sheet.
She wins an unusual amount. There are whispers. She is not cheating; she is just that good.
**Risk:** 🟢 Low (sweet, loyal; would never say a word)
**Money:** $$ (buys all the bingo cards, buys extra daubers to sell to newcomers)
**Dialogue:** *"B-7! Oh Lord, B-7, I have been waiting for you all night, come to mama."*
**Special event:** "Darlene's Streak" — she wins 4 in a row during a session. The room
erupts. Heat goes up 10 from the cheering but morale is so high that everyone tips 25% more.

---

### 3. Cletus Boone — "The Loud Winner"
**Behavior:** Plays slots and bingo. Has no volume control. His joy is genuine, infectious,
and approximately as quiet as a lawnmower. His wins cause the most noise-related Heat spikes
in the game.
**Risk:** 🔴 High (noise, heat, general chaos)
**Money:** $$$ (spends generously; wins and loses big)
**Dialogue:** *"BINGO! THAT'S BINGO! I GOT BINGO! Y'ALL HEAR THAT?! BINGO!!!"*
**Special event:** "Cletus Meltdown" — Cletus wins a jackpot during a Heat-critical moment.
Player must use Hush Now or Emergency Bingo Cover within 10 seconds or Heat jumps +20.

---

### 4. Minnie Vashti — "The Poker Shark"
**Behavior:** Retired math teacher. Plays Texas Hold'em like she's grading papers.
Silent, precise, devastating. Rarely celebrates a win visibly. Losses are greeted with
a slight nod and an immediate rebuy. She is reading everyone at the table.
**Risk:** 🟡 Medium (discreet; but wins are so large other players sometimes complain loudly)
**Money:** $$$$$ (she's profitable for the house because she keeps everyone at the table longer)
**Dialogue:** *"I'll raise. You can fold now or in two minutes — the math works out the same."*
**Special event:** "The Minnie Effect" — if Minnie is at the poker table, all other poker
players stay 30% longer and spend 20% more. Running the poker table while Minnie is there
gives +$300 bonus per session.

---

### 5. The Nervous Newbie
**Name archetype:** First-timer, referred by a regular
**Behavior:** Awkward, doesn't know the etiquette. Asks too many questions. Almost knocks
over the snack bar. Probably going to become a regular if they have a good time tonight.
**Risk:** 🟡 Medium (nervous = unpredictable; might blurt something in front of wrong person)
**Money:** $$ (overspends slightly from anxiety; tips out of guilt)
**Dialogue:** *"So this is like… legal, right? Because my cousin said it was fine
but I just wanted to— okay, yeah, no, it's fine. I'm fine. What do I do first?"*
**Special event:** "Conversion Night" — if the Newbie has a good time (bingo win,
friendly regular at their table), they become a Tier 1 Regular. If they lose badly or
Cletus scares them, they leave and don't return.

---

### 6. The Lucky Cowboy
**Name archetype:** Buck / Travis / Wyatt
**Behavior:** Comes in wearing an actual hat. Loud but charming. Wins big one in every
four visits. Losses are met with a shrug and the phrase *"Well, shoot."* Beloved by
the room.
**Risk:** 🟡 Medium (fun energy but not always subtle)
**Money:** $$$
**Dialogue:** *"Dealer, I am feeling something special tonight. Something in the air.
You feel that? That's luck. And luck smells like dryer sheets."*
**Special event:** "Cowboy Hat Lucky" — when the Lucky Cowboy wins big, it buffs the entire
room's morale for 20 minutes: all other customers tip 10% more and Noise Meter decays faster.

---

### 7. The Fake Rich Guy
**Name archetype:** Dennis / Whitmore / Skip
**Behavior:** Dresses like he has money. Bets like he has money. Definitely does not have
money. Stiffs on tips. Occasionally tries to run a tab.
**Risk:** 🟡 Medium (creates social tension; other regulars know he's broke)
**Money:** $$ (nominal; sometimes $0 after losses)
**Dialogue:** *"Put it on my account. I have an account here, right? I'll set one up now."*
**Special event:** "Running a Tab" — Dennis asks to run a tab. Player choice:
A) Approve it: Risk of no payment, Heat +0, Roy is disgusted.
B) Decline politely: Dennis is embarrassed but pays cash. Safe outcome.
C) Make Roy collect it for you: +Heat 2, Roy is delighted with this task.

---

### 8. The Sore Loser
**Name archetype:** Gary / Phil / Randy
**Behavior:** Plays poker. Loses. Becomes convinced he was cheated. Becomes increasingly
loud about this. Is never cheated — he is just a bad poker player.
**Risk:** 🔴 High (anger escalation; might cause a scene near the front of the building)
**Money:** $$$ (spends well; but negates earnings with chaos)
**Dialogue:** *"That machine is rigged. This table is rigged. You are all in on it.
I have NEVER lost on a flush, not ONCE in my LIFE, and I—"*
**Special event:** "Sore Loser Meltdown" — Gary reaches a breaking point. Player must use
Grandma Stare + 1 drink token to calm him down, OR let the security guy escort him out
(-Heat short term, but Gary tells a friend, -Backroom reputation). Best outcome: let
Minnie Vashti calmly explain the odds to him. He goes quiet.

---

### 9. The Snack Thief
**Name archetype:** Tater / Billy Ray / Bugs
**Behavior:** Comes for the snacks more than the gambling. Grabs handfuls from the snack
bar without paying. Technically he buys one thing and "samples" everything else. He's
friendly and everyone likes him too much to say anything.
**Risk:** 🟢 Low (harmless; just expensive to stock the snack bar)
**Money:** $ (pays almost nothing)
**Dialogue:** *"These peanuts are incredible, Retta. Have you tried your own peanuts?
You should try your own peanuts."*
**Special event:** "Empty Snack Bar" — if Snack Thief visits during a high-traffic night,
the snack bar depletes 3× faster. Other customers get annoyed. Restock is urgent.
Player can assign Darlene to the snack bar to manage portions.

---

### 10. The High Roller
**Name archetype:** Mr. V / "Big Mac" / The Accountant
**Behavior:** Shows up once or twice a chapter. Sits at the VIP table. Plays quietly for
two hours. Wins or loses with equal serenity. Leaves a tip that covers the night's expenses.
Never tells anyone his actual name.
**Risk:** 🟢 Low (discreet, professional, oddly reassuring)
**Money:** $$$$$ (single visit can equal 5 regular nights)
**Dialogue:** *"Your setup is impressive, Mrs. Hollis. I appreciate the discretion.
Same time next month."*
**Special event:** "High Roller Visit" — when he arrives (random, unannounced), the VIP
corner must be stocked and a staff member assigned. If anything goes wrong during his visit,
he leaves early and doesn't return for two chapters. If everything is perfect, he leaves a
$500 bonus.

---

### 11. The Bingo Grandma Posse
**Name archetype:** A group of 4–5 older ladies (Edna, June, Carlene, Faye, Hattie)
**Behavior:** Come as a group every bingo night. They've been playing bingo together since
1987 and have their own seating arrangement that is NOT negotiable. They bring their own
daubers. They bring their own snacks (Retta taxes these by 10%).
**Risk:** 🟢 Low (well-behaved; just very loud collectively)
**Money:** $$$ (group buy-in; they also buy from the snack bar extensively)
**Dialogue:** *"Edna! That's B-12! EDNA! She can't hear. EDNA, B-12! TWELVE, EDNA!"*
**Special event:** "Grandma Posse vs. Cletus" — when the Grandma Posse and Cletus are in
the same bingo session, the noise competition between their competing victory celebrations
doubles the Noise Meter fill rate. Extremely profitable. Extremely loud.

---

### 12. The Conspiracy Regular
**Name archetype:** Carl / Vern / Lester
**Behavior:** Plays cards. Believes the slot machine is talking to him. Keeps a notebook.
Does not trust the new slot machine but plays it anyway "to gather data."
**Risk:** 🟡 Medium (mostly harmless but sometimes says weird things at volume)
**Money:** $$ (mid-range)
**Dialogue:** *"Three cherries again. You know what three cherries means in numerology?
It means the machine knows me. I've told you this, Retta."*
**Special event:** "Vern's Theory" — Vern accosts a new player with his theory that the
bingo board is psychic. The new player either: A) agrees enthusiastically (both are happy,
Heat +2), or B) looks to Retta for confirmation. Retta's response is a masterpiece of
misdirection.

---

### 13. The Suspicious Preacher-Type Visitor
**Name archetype:** "Brother" Norwood / Reverend-Adjacent Gerald / The Volunteer
**Behavior:** Shows up not to gamble but to "check on folks." He's heard rumors. He's
concerned. He's also, privately, curious. He sits at the edge of the room nursing a sweet
tea and watching everyone very carefully.
**Risk:** 🔴 High (heat source; reports to the church network; Connie knows him)
**Money:** $ (barely; occasionally plays bingo once, badly)
**Dialogue:** *"I'm just here out of community interest. I like to know what folks are
doing. Nothing wrong with being interested. Is that a slot machine? I'm just observing."*
**Special event:** "The Visitor Problem" — if he stays for 20 minutes without being
redirected, Heat +15 and he writes a prayer request about "certain establishments."
Best counter: deploy Family Emergency or get Roy Macabee to talk to him (they know each other).

---

### 14. The Off-Duty Deputy (Not Clem)
**Name archetype:** Deputy Santos / Officer Hayes
**Behavior:** Not Clem. The other deputy. He doesn't know about the backroom. He came
in because someone told him the bingo was fun. He is having the time of his life. He
also shouldn't be here. This is a wildcard.
**Risk:** 🔴 High (uniform makes everyone nervous; technically he's off-duty and just playing bingo)
**Money:** $$ (pays his entry, buys drinks)
**Dialogue:** *"I'm off the clock! I'm allowed to have hobbies! Where do I get a dauber?!"*
**Special event:** "Friendly Cop Problem" — the room freezes when he enters. Player must
decide: let him stay (risk he notices something, but he becomes a friendly buffer against
Heat), or use Family Emergency to get him out (safe, but he's confused and mildly offended).

---

### 15. The Champion Gambler
**Name archetype:** "Big Win" Bernie / Dallas / Ace
**Behavior:** Traveled 45 minutes to hear about this place. Plays everything. Wins frequently
enough that Roy finds him unsettling. Talks to strangers. Buys rounds of drinks. Absolutely
the best energy in the room and simultaneously the worst for keeping a low profile.
**Risk:** 🔴 High (visibility, noise, encourages others to cheer)
**Money:** $$$$$ (massive spender; massive winner)
**Dialogue:** *"WHERE has this place been all my life?! RETTA, you are a GENIUS, I am telling
EVERYONE I know about this— wait, should I tell everyone? What's the protocol here?"*
**Special event:** "The Champion's Night" — when the Champion wins big, the entire room
cheers. Noise Meter spikes instantly. Retta must activate Hush Now and calm the room in
under 8 seconds or Heat jumps +25. Worth it financially if managed correctly.

---

### 16. The Regulars' Kid
**Name archetype:** Marcus Jr. / Little Roy / Baby Cletus (not actually a baby)
**Behavior:** Technically too young to be here. Roy brought him once because the babysitter

**Risk:** 🟢 Low (everyone watches out for him)
**Money:** $ (very low)

**Special event:** "Kid's First Win" — the first time Little Roy wins a hand, the whole room
celebrates. +Morale for all regulars. Retta gives him the poker chip as a "souvenir."
Reputation +15.

---

### 17. The Rival Spy
**Name archetype:** Tammy from Wade's / One of Wade's guys
**Behavior:** Sent by Wade Pruett to see what the buzz is about. Terrible spy. Awkward. Keeps
taking notes on her phone. Doesn't know the secret knock. Got in because she followed Roy
through the alley and Roy felt bad for her.
**Risk:** 🔴 High (she will absolutely tell Wade everything she saw)
**Money:** $ (not actually here to gamble)
