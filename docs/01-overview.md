# Sections 1–3: Overview, Gameplay Loop & Player Actions

---

## Section 1: High Concept

### Elevator Pitch

**Wash & Wager** is a mobile idle tycoon game about Loretta "Retta" Hollis — a sharp, sweet,
and slightly terrifying Texas grandma who spends her life savings on the most run-down
laundromat in Peavine County. On the surface, she's just trying to keep the washers running
and the floor dry. Behind the "Employees Only" door, she's quietly running the most
entertaining Wednesday night in town.

The core fantasy:
> *A sassy grandma buys a boring laundromat, turns it into the town's weirdest money
> machine, and tries to keep the front looking normal while the backroom gets crazier
> every week.*

### Why This Game Is Fun

**1. The Double Life Tension.**
Every dollar you earn has two lives: the clean public laundromat where you wave at the
deputy and smile at church ladies, and the warm neon chaos behind the fake storage shelf.
Balancing both is the core tension. A washing machine overflow is annoying. A washing machine
overflow *during a bingo jackpot when the deputy is at the front counter* is a masterpiece.

**2. Eatventure-style Satisfaction.**
The front laundromat is an idle machine that clicks, hums, and grows. Coins pop. Dryers spin.
Customers queue and wait and fold. Watching it run smoother and faster as you upgrade is
immediately satisfying — and it's your cover story.

**3. The Backroom Escalation Arc.**
The backroom starts as a card table and a folding chair. By the end it has mood lighting,
a slot machine, a secret hallway, and a VIP corner where Roy Macabee cries every Tuesday.
Each expansion is a comedy setpiece.

**4. Grandma Energy.**
Retta is a character you want to spend time with. She has lines for everything. She stares
down the health inspector with a sweet tea in one hand and a "bless your heart" on her lips.
She is simultaneously the most wholesome and most dangerous person in Peavine County.

**5. The Town Cast.**
A rotating cast of small-town regulars — the gossipy neighbor, the suspicious deputy, the
pompous rival laundromat owner, the preacher who shows up at the worst times — creates a
sitcom stage where chaos is always just one bad timing away.

**6. Mobile Idle Progression.**
Numbers go up. Machines unlock. Stars appear. Customers multiply. Upgrades cascade.
The game respects your time when you're away (idle income) and rewards active play
(event decisions, Retta abilities, repair chains). It feels like progress even if you
only play for three minutes.

## Section 2: Core Gameplay Loop

### The Macro Loop (Big Picture)

```
More customers → More income → More upgrades → More customers
         ↕                                          ↕
    Fix problems                          Unlock backroom
         ↕                                          ↕
  Manage heat                           Balance front/back
```

### The Micro Loop (Each Session)

**Step 1 — Customers Arrive**
Customers walk in from the front entrance. They're all locals: construction workers,
college kids, tired moms, retired veterans. They choose an available washing machine,
load their laundry, insert coins, and start the cycle. The player does NOT personally
wash each load — the customers do their own thing.

**Step 2 — Customers Use Machines**
- A customer walks to an empty washer and pays to start it (coin animation plays).
- The washer runs a timer (shorter with upgrades, longer at base level).
- If all washers are taken, the customer waits near the entrance. Patience meter counts down.
- While waiting, they might visit the vending machine, the detergent shelf, or the folding tables.

**Step 3 — Problems Appear**
Randomly (on a timer, weighted by machine age and upgrade level) problems spawn:
- **Machine breakdown** — washer/dryer shows a wrench icon and stops working.
- **Spill** — detergent puddle, coffee spill, or overflow appears on the floor.
- **Lint trap full** — dryer slows and eventually stops.
- **Vending empty** — no more snacks/drinks, customers get annoyed.
- **Detergent shelf empty** — customers can't buy their own detergent.
- **Coin machine jammed** — customers can't get coins, get angry.
- **Customer complaint** — impatient customer produces an exclamation bubble; tap to calm.

**Step 4 — Player Collects & Repairs**
The player (as Retta, walking around the map) can:
- Tap coin bags to collect them (or auto-collect with upgrades).
- Tap broken machines to start a repair timer (or send the repair guy).
- Tap spills to mop (or send a cleaner).
- Tap the vending machine when the restock icon appears.
- Tap angry customers to calm them down (costs a charm token or uses a Retta ability).

**Step 5 — Spend Money on Upgrades**
Between active sessions, or from the upgrade menu, the player spends coins on:
- Machine upgrades (faster cycles, bigger capacity, less breakdown chance).
- Building expansions (new rooms, better waiting area, decorations).
- Staff hires and upgrades.
- Atmosphere items that increase customer patience and spending.

**Step 6 — Story Advances → Backroom Unlocks**
At a set chapter milestone (Chapter 4), Retta discovers the storage room has a
suspiciously solid back wall. Tapping it enough times reveals a hidden door.
The backroom begins as one card table. From here, a parallel loop begins:

**The Backroom Loop (from Chapter 4+)**
```
Backroom customers arrive (via alley) → They gamble/play → They pay → Income
              ↕                                                          ↕
        Heat rises                                            Upgrade backroom
              ↕                                                          ↕
    Cover-up events trigger                               New games unlock
              ↕                                                          ↕
  Manage both rooms at once                         Balance heat vs. income
```

**Step 7 — Heat Events**
Once the backroom is running, the Heat Meter (0–100) becomes active.
Noise, unusual foot traffic, big winnings shouted too loud, and nosy neighbors
all raise the heat. The player must:
- Keep the front laundromat looking clean and busy (lowers heat passively).
- Respond to heat events (deputy at the door, gossip asking questions).
- Use Retta's abilities to defuse situations.
- Upgrade the backroom (soundproofing, better hidden door, lookout cameras).

**Heat vs. Income Trade-off:**
Running more backroom games = more income = more heat.
Running a cleaner, quieter front = less heat.
The fun is in threading the needle.

### Eatventure Parallels

| Eatventure Mechanic | Wash & Wager Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Customer buys food, chef cooks | Customer loads washer, washer runs timer |
| Restaurant stations overflow | Machines break, spills appear |
| Upgrade kitchen stations | Upgrade washers/dryers/backroom tables |
| Hire staff (cashier, cook) | Hire cleaner, repair guy, backroom host |
| Floor space expands | New rooms unlock (lounge, backroom, VIP) |
| Stars / level meter fills | Chapter progress + Reputation bar |
| VIP customers | Backroom regulars (Roy, the High Roller, etc.) |

**Key difference from Eatventure:** In Wash & Wager the player does *not* serve
each customer manually. The laundromat is self-service. Retta's role is reactive:
fix problems, collect money, make decisions, and run her side business.

## Section 3: Player Actions

Everything the player can directly do as Retta:

### Movement & Navigation
- **Walk** around the laundromat and (eventually) backroom by tapping a destination.
- **Enter / exit backroom** via the hidden door (tap the fake storage shelf or use the
  secret knock button after it's installed).
- **Switch between rooms** (front view ↔ backroom view) via the bottom navigation tabs.

### Money Collection
- **Collect coins** — tap coin bags floating above coin machines, vending machines, and
  washers/dryers (auto-collector upgrades remove the need to tap).
- **Collect backroom income** — tap chips/cash bags above the card table, bingo board,
  slot machines, etc.
- **Collect tips** — tap tip jars at the front counter.

### Maintenance
- **Repair machines** — tap a broken washer/dryer to start Retta's repair animation
  (3–10 seconds depending on upgrades; or dispatch the repair guy).
- **Mop spills** — tap a spill puddle to trigger the mop animation.
- **Empty lint traps** — tap a full lint trap icon on dryers.
- **Refill vending machines** — tap the empty vending machine when the restock icon appears.
- **Restock detergent shelf** — tap the empty shelf.
- **Unjam coin machine** — tap the coin machine when the jam icon appears.

### Customer Management
- **Calm angry customer** — tap a customer with a red exclamation to queue a calming action
  (costs a Sweet Talk token; refills over time). Increases customer patience.
- **Redirect waiting customer** — tap a waiting customer and tap a newly freed machine to
  speed up their path.
- **Boost impatient customer** — tap a customer about to leave to use a "Free Detergent" 
  coupon (costs 1 coupon, refills daily).

### Staff Management
- **Assign tasks** — open the staff menu, drag a staff portrait to a task or room area.
- **Call staff to location** — tap a problem area while a relevant staff member is idle to
  dispatch them.
- **Level up staff** — in the Staff menu, spend coins to upgrade a staff member's tier.
- **Hire new staff** — open staff slots as the building expands.

### Grandma Abilities (Active)
Retta has a skill bar with cooldown abilities (detailed in Section 15):
- **Sweet Tea Distraction** — lures an inconvenient NPC away from a sensitive area.
- **Grandma Stare** — freezes a problem customer or nosy visitor in their tracks.
- **Coupon Confusion** — overwhelms an inspector/deputy with paperwork, buying time.
- **Emergency Bingo Cover** — instantly spawns a "legitimate bingo night" facade in the back.
- **Fake Repair Mode** — covers up a gambling machine as a "broken appliance."
- **Bless Your Heart** — reduces a customer's anger to zero and earns a small tip.
- **Hush Now** — mutes all backroom sound effects for 60 seconds (reduces heat gain).
- **Family Emergency** — sends a nosy NPC away for an entire day.
- **Clean Sweep** — Retta mops, repairs, and restocks everything in one dramatic rush.

### Backroom Management (Chapter 4+)
- **Start bingo round** — tap the bingo board to initiate a bingo session.
- **Deal poker** — tap the poker table to start a round.
- **Spin the lucky wheel** — tap the wheel to activate (cooldown-based).
- **Pull slots** — tap the slot machine (customer-operated, but Retta can trigger a bonus pull).
- **Check lookout camera** — tap the camera monitor to see if someone suspicious is approaching.
- **Activate fake wall** — tap the hidden door button to close/lock the backroom entrance.
- **Handle heat events** — respond to dialogue popups that require a choice.

### Story & Progression
- **Read story dialogue** — tap speech bubbles from NPCs during story events.
- **Make event decisions** — choose options A, B, or C in dialogue events (Section 13 has 40 of these).
- **Complete chapter goals** — track progress in the Chapter menu; collect chapter rewards.
- **Accept quests** — tap the quest board to view active quests and claim completed rewards.
